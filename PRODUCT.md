# Ona — Product Context

## Product Summary
Ona is an open-source physical-to-digital art provenance protocol and sales engine built for emerging African and global fine artists. Buyers purchase physical artworks by card/bank transfer (Paystack NGN, Stripe USD) or mobile stablecoins (Celo MiniPay `cUSD`, Base `USDC`) with zero wallet setup; each physical piece carries a tamper-evident NXP NTAG 424 DNA NFC seal paired to an on-chain ERC-721 + ERC-2981 authenticity certificate and a Filecoin/IPFS CIDv1 provenance bundle.

## Primary Users
1. **Emerging Fine Artists (Lagos, Accra, Nairobi):** Painters, sculptors, and printmakers currently selling via Instagram/WhatsApp without verifiable provenance, cross-border checkout, or secondary resale royalties.
2. **Art Collectors & Galleries Worldwide:** Buyers who want cryptographic proof of authenticity and ownership history without managing seed phrases or gas tokens.
3. **Ecosystem & Grant Reviewers (Karma GAP, Base, Celo, Filecoin, Tezos, Flow):** Technical evaluators inspecting smart contracts, NFC replay protection, walletless checkout, and enforced royalty settlement.
