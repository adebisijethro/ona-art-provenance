# Ona — Visual System & Design Direction

## Visual World
**Archival Museum Catalogue & Intaglio Provenance Ledger.**  
Warm cotton rag paper (`#F6F2EA`), mat-board linen (`#EDE7DB`), carbon intaglio ink (`#181512`), terracotta wax-seal vermilion (`#9C3B24`), and archival forest verification ink (`#1B4D3E`). Built for daylight gallery viewing where physical artworks lead and cryptographic telemetry reads like an engraved curatorial colophon.

## Typography
- **Display & Artwork Titles:** `Cormorant Garamond` — classic fine-art press & auction catalogue display serif.
- **Interface & Body Copy:** `Hanken Grotesk` — legible editorial grotesque at 15–17px with 65–72ch measure.
- **Cryptographic Ledger, Hashes & Currency:** `IBM Plex Mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` — reserved strictly for CIDv1 multihashes, NTAG 424 DNA counters, EVM addresses, and financial splits.

## Craft Guarantees
- Contrast ≥ 6.8:1 for secondary warm-umber copy (`#544C43` on `#F6F2EA`) and ≥ 14.5:1 for primary ink.
- Zero eyebrows/kickers above headings, zero gradient text, zero violet, zero image hover scaling, zero emoji icons.
- Custom browser chrome: themed selection, caret, scrollbars, focus-visible rings, and tabular numerals.
